# Writing Fiction

Assume familiarity with ordinary story craft. Concentrate on the recurring failures that survive competent prose: early disclosure, over-explained meaning, single-track causality, stock invention, and long-form drift.

**Core principle:** Control narrative information and state before polishing sentences. Surface de-slopping cannot repair a predictable or over-resolved story.

**CRITICAL precedence and loading**

- Treat the user's manuscript, canon, voice, viewpoint, genre, and requested outcome as authoritative.
- Before novel, series, multi-chapter continuation, or roughly 10,000-plus-word work, read [references/continuity-ledger.md](references/continuity-ledger.md).
- For revision, critique, or a final draft check, read the sections of [references/diagnostic-audit.md](references/diagnostic-audit.md) relevant to the requested outcome.

## Route by scale and task

Treat **fiction** as the umbrella, a **story** as one complete narrative unit, and a **novel** as a long narrative whose state must survive many drafting sessions.

| Request | Working unit | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Flash or short story | Whole story | A stated lesson or fully sealed ending |
| Scene or chapter | Scene plus adjacent context | Local polish that ignores what changes |
| Novella | Sequence of scenes | Episodic middle and flat pressure |
| Novel or series | Chapter plus external state | Voice, canon, disclosure, and causal drift |
| Continuation | Supplied text plus next unit | Mimicking cadence while violating facts or genre |
| Revision or critique | Existing draft | Synonym edits that preserve structural faults |

## Establish the story contract

Extract explicit user constraints first.

For new work, write a compact internal packet:

```text
PROMISE     What experience or genre satisfaction must be delivered?
QUESTION    What live uncertainty pulls the reader through the next unit?
WITHHELD    What is unavailable, why, and when can it be paid out?
COST        What is the best case for the choice the focal character refuses?
VOICE       60-100 words or 3-5 observable traits from the target prose
BOUNDARY    What must not change: POV, tense, realism, tone, rating, canon
DEFAULTS    First-choice names, jobs, settings, objects, and twists to reconsider
```

Do not ask the user for decisions already recoverable from supplied text. Ask only when different answers would produce materially different books.

For a continuation, derive the packet from the excerpt before adding plot. Track who knows what. Treat unexplained facts as intentionally unknown rather than invitations to overwrite them.

For long-form work, use the continuity ledger as durable state rather than passive notes. Follow its full cycle: locate or reconstruct it, open a current-unit brief, draft, audit the finished unit, then update the ledger from on-page evidence before continuing. On resumption, finish the first incomplete ledger step instead of redrafting completed work.

## Draft through disclosure

Build each scene around a change in state, then decide what the reader receives.

1. Enter with a live question or unstable expectation.
2. Change at least two of these: desire, knowledge, risk, relationship, constraint, or reader prediction.
3. Separate event from disclosure. A thing may happen before the reader understands it.
4. Keep information asymmetry alive between characters, the reader, or both.
5. Confirm late. Attraction, suspicion, identity, motive, or danger loses pressure once made explicit.
6. Make a revelation alter the meaning of an earlier detail when the form supports it. A surprise that changes only what happens next is thin.
7. Exit with changed options, not an explanatory paragraph.

At roughly three-quarters of a complete story, list three plausible endings from the evidence already given. If only one remains, or the planned ending is the obvious first answer, move a confirmation later, restore an alternative interpretation, or let a secondary consequence remain live.

## Counter recurrent convergence

Treat these as diagnostic priors, not bans:

- **Meaning explained twice.** The scene dramatizes an idea, then narration or dialogue states it. Remove the explanation. If the idea disappears, strengthen the event rather than the commentary.
- **Emotion performed through the body.** Tightened chests, held breath, hammering pulses, metallic tastes, and weather mirroring mood become a default channel. Mix plain emotion labels, choices, avoidance, misdirected attention, dialogue, and sometimes no interpretation.
- **Everything serves the main plot.** Give a secondary line its own desire and consequence. It may rhyme with the main action without existing to deliver clues.
- **Clean agency.** The focal character causes the solution and gains the correct insight. Allow institutions, luck, weather, another person's private agenda, or irreversible timing to shape outcomes, unless genre requires focal agency.
- **Chronology as a conveyor belt.** Use a time move only when placement changes the reader's interpretation. Decorative flashbacks add complexity without recontextualization.
- **Moral tidiness.** State the strongest case for the rejected option. If it is weak, the choice is an errand.
- **Sealed generic world.** Prefer details that arise from place, class, work, era, or character history. Do not add random brands or facts merely to look specific.
- **Default invention.** Reconsider the first plausible name, profession, setting, heirloom, weather image, and revelation.
- **Polished closure.** A final realization, aphorism, epilogue, or total thread closure often over-finishes the work. End on the form's actual promise, then leave chosen residue.

Genre promises win. Romance still owes its central emotional resolution. A fair-play mystery must solve the case fairly. A thriller usually requires decisive agency at the climax. Deliberate moral instruction may belong in children's forms. Apply the diagnostics where the genre contract is silent.

## Preserve voice without copying a costume

Model voice as constraints, not adjectives:

- sentence-length range and clause habits
- psychic distance and what the viewpoint notices
- diction boundary, including words the voice would never choose
- paragraph movement and transition habits
- humor, judgment, and tolerance for explanation
- figurative domains already used

Continue the noticing and judgment system, not merely punctuation. A close cadence match with a new omniscient fact, unexplained genre shift, or impossible character knowledge is still voice failure.

When revising another writer, preserve idiosyncrasy, roughness, repetitions, and deliberate symmetry unless they obstruct the requested effect. Do not normalize the manuscript into this skill's default register.

## Revise by re-deriving

Re-derive a scene only when a structural defect affects the requested outcome. For wording, voice, or local edits, revise the affected passage directly and check only relevant continuity constraints. Before a structural rewrite, state:

```text
ENTRY STATE   What each relevant character wants, knows, and risks
TURN          The event or disclosure that changes the available choices
EXIT STATE    What is now possible, impossible, or newly costly
CARRY         The uncertainty passed to the next unit
```

For a structural rewrite, draft the scene again from those functions before line editing; substitutions alone do not repair structural defects.

Apply only checks relevant to the requested change. Complete any needed structural revision before the prose pass. Count or quote findings; an unenumerated “looks fine” is not a check. Use the relevant sections of [references/diagnostic-audit.md](references/diagnostic-audit.md).

## Output contract

- When asked to write fiction, return the fiction without an assistant preamble, craft lecture, summary, or trailing offer.
- When asked to outline, expose only the planning detail requested; preserve useful unknowns.
- When asked to critique, report quoted evidence and craft effects. Do not assign an authorship probability.
- When asked to revise, return revised prose unless the user also requests notes.
- When asked to continue, do not recap the supplied passage.
- Do not invent factual claims about the real world to satisfy specificity.

## Compact example

Draft:

> When the mayor called the missing ledger blue, Vale realized he had stolen it. A chill ran down her spine. His mistake told her everything.

Diagnosis: private knowledge becomes certainty too quickly, the bodily reaction adds nothing, and “everything” closes explanations the evidence does not eliminate.

```text
ENTRY STATE   Vale suspects the mayor but lacks usable evidence
TURN          He reveals knowledge that was never made public
EXIT STATE    She changes tactics without revealing his mistake
CARRY         How he learned the color, and whether he noticed the slip
```

Re-derived:

> “The blue ledger burned with the rest,” the mayor said.
>
> Vale closed her notebook. The surviving clerk had called it a cashbook. No one had mentioned the cover.
>
> “Tell me again,” she said, “which window you used to see the fire.”

The same fact now functions as a fair clue. It changes Vale's behavior while preserving live explanations for the mayor's knowledge.

## Common mistakes and recovery

| Mistake | Recovery |
|---|---|
| Replacing “AI words” while leaving the same disclosure schedule | Return to QUESTION, WITHHELD, and the three-quarter forecast |
| Adding random specificity | Derive detail from character access, place, work, and consequence |
| Making an ending ambiguous by withholding the genre payoff | Deliver the promise; leave residue elsewhere |
| Treating every diagnostic as a prohibition | Preserve purposeful craft and note why it earns the pattern |
| Letting a continuation become a new genre | Reassert BOUNDARY and remove unsupported machinery |
| Carrying a novel in context alone | Rebuild and maintain the continuity ledger |
| “Fixing” an author's established voice | Restore their observable constraints and change only what the request targets |

If a diagnostic fix would damage canon, voice, or genre, keep the story contract and report the tradeoff when notes are requested.
